# OCRing Laws

These laws codify OCRing core governance and policy invariants. They are architectural laws, not UI preferences.

1. Pixels are evidence, not inventory.
2. OCR output is observation, not truth.
3. AI interprets and allocates; profiles constrain meaning.
4. Confidence is not correctness.
5. Correlated evidence is not counted as independent evidence.
6. Hard contradictions can veto weighted confidence.
7. Unknown is preferable to an invented answer.
8. Capture must not normally wait for extraction.
9. Preserve information before preserving redundant frames.
10. The 3 GB memory ceiling is hard.
11. Verified inventory is never silently overwritten.
12. Profile evolution never rewrites historical provenance.
13. User verification outranks automated interpretation for that record.
14. Game-specific meaning stays outside OCRing Core.
15. No hidden game-process access is required.
16. Proven overlap does not by itself prove full-inventory completeness.
17. Review is a first-class resolution path, not a failure state.
18. Degradation must preserve evidence and provenance before convenience.
